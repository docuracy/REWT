# The rebuilt landscapes

[← REWT](../index)

The source programme in [Evidence](../evidence) is general. Where it has to be worked out
in detail is the handful of landscapes whose hydrography was **rebuilt rather than
modified** — and in which, therefore, the modern channel is the *weakest* evidence of a
premodern course.

**A tenth of England lies inside an [Internal Drainage District](https://www.ada.org.uk/){:target="_blank"}: 12,779 km of mapped
watercourse, 13.3% of the network.** That is the measurable extent of the problem, and
these pages divide it up.

## What connects them

In every case the question is the same and the answer is local: **which channels are
older than they look, and which are engineering?** National pattern rules — dating a
channel by whether it is called a *drain*, a *cut*, a *sewer* — answer it crudely and
should admit as much. Each region below has a literature that answers it properly for
that region, and the work is to bring those answers into the dataset one region at a
time.

Which is also why [phased release](../epochs) matters: no edition need wait for all of
them.
